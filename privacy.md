# Privacy Policy — Personal Drive Backup

Last updated: 9 November 2026

**Who uses the app.** Personal Drive Backup is used by one person only — the owner of this
page — to back up his own files to his own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public.

**What Google data the app accesses.** Only the files and folders in the owner's Google Drive
that the app itself created (Google scope `drive.file`). The app does not read other Drive files,
email, contacts or any other Google data.

**How the data is used.** Files are copied from the owner's computer to his Google Drive and
compared with the copies there, to keep the backup complete. Nothing else is done with them.

**Storage and sharing.** The data stays on the owner's computer and in his Google Drive. It is
not sent to any other server, not shared with or sold to anyone, and not used for advertising
or for training AI models. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer.

**Revoking access.** Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account:
Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services.

**Contact.** Through the GitHub profile that hosts this page.
